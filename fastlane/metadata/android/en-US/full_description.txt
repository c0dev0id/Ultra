<p><i>Olauncher Light</i> is an ultra minimal and open source launcher. It respects your privacy and helps you with living a distraction free digital life.</p><p>You should use your phone, not the other way round.</p><p><br><b>FEATURES:</b></p><p><b>Gestures:</b></p><ul><li>Swipe up for app list</li><li>Swipe down for notification drawer</li><li>Swipe right to open phone app</li><li>Swipe left for camera</li><li>Long press anywhere to change home app alignment</li><li>Double tap for lock option</li></ul><p>Note: If fingerprint/face unlock doesn't work, double tap on the screen to wake it up. This happens because the app uses screen timeout, which takes a few seconds to lock the phone.</p><p><br><b>Apps:</b></p><ul><li>Long press home apps to change them</li><li>Tap to launch</li><li>Swipe up and search for quick launch</li><li>Long press on apps in list for more info</li></ul>
